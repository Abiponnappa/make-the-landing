# Make the Landing

> A sponsor-revenue engine designed to create repeatable NIL funding opportunities for Utah Red Rocks gymnasts.

🏆 Third Place — Adobe Red Rocks AI Innovation Challenge, 2026

## The challenge

Red Rocks needs sustainable NIL funding to attract, retain, and empower championship-caliber student-athletes. Local businesses may want to support athletes, but NIL partnerships can feel difficult to discover, launch, and measure.

## The solution

Make the Landing helps local sponsors:

- Discover Red Rocks athletes and their partnership value
- Build campaigns based on business goals and budget
- Receive athlete and campaign recommendations
- Use campaign-ready creative and trackable QR offers
- Measure performance signals that support sponsor renewal

The result is a repeatable model: sponsor investment → athlete-approved campaign → measurable results → renewal → more NIL opportunity.

## MVP features

- Athlete directory and individualized athlete profiles
- Sponsor campaign builder with $500, $1,500, $3,000, and custom options
- Athlete-fit campaign recommendations
- Athlete approval workflow
- Adobe Express-style campaign creative kit
- Sponsor ROI dashboard with reach, engagement, QR scans, and redemptions
- NIL Portal showing how repeat sponsor campaigns can grow team-wide funding

## Business model

The MVP begins with a focused local-sponsor pilot. Sponsors invest in campaigns that provide measurable business value; successful campaigns are designed to earn renewal and create repeatable NIL funding.

All financial figures and campaign performance metrics in the prototype are illustrative.
